# Opening artwork: separate, airy depth planes

The 2026-10-04 revision separates all hero botanicals and brass from the architectural background. The final direction is sparse and restrained, with generous ivory space around the names and portrait. Created using the **built-in image_gen tool**; original output PNGs are preserved. WebP derivatives retain alpha where appropriate.

The three botanical images each contain disjoint left/right groups on a transparent 1536 × 1024 canvas. CSS splits each at the clear central line into six independent wings, avoiding stretched imagery. Canopy sits farthest back, jasmine closer, low foliage closest. Two smaller brass bells have their own translation and gentle rotation around the chain top. The architecture moves slowly behind them.

Phones/tablets anchor natural-aspect botanical planes to the top and bottom instead of cropping away their sides. At phone widths, the bells end above the names and navigation has a quiet ivory backing. Native scrolling remains free; reduced-motion and Pause motion show the complete still arrangement. Existing other chapters, their photographs and closing illustration are retained.

The denser `hero-botanicals-v2` asset is an unconsumed intermediate retained for provenance. It is not requested by the final page. The original combined `hero-garden` and original botanical illustration remain unchanged.
