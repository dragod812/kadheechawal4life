Our story

A little crush.
A whole life.

Before they knew it was a love story,
it was a school crush.

There were songs Sidharth wrote for Kalyani, recordings sent her way, and two people taking their sweet time to work out the obvious.

They kept talking through the years. Then came Mumbai, a broken leg, and a walk along Marine Drive. Kalyani held his hand. Technically, for balance. As it turned out, for life.

These days, life stretches between Mumbai and San Francisco, with calls across time zones. When they’re together, there is music: she sings, he plays guitar and joins in.

Every reunion turns the colour up. More laughter, more adventure, and days when doing nothing together feels like enough. Each time, they know more surely: this is the life they want.

He wrote for her again.
And asked her to marry him.
She said yes.
